我們收集什麼

為了調整遊戲平衡，遊戲會把匿名的遊玩紀錄傳到開發者自己的伺服器：

  • 遊玩內容：選的角色與皮膚、難度、每關的得分與是否過關、買賣的裝備、遊玩時間等。
  • 遊戲版本、作業系統與手機型號（例如 iPhone15,2）。
  • 一個安裝時隨機產生的代號，只用來把同一支手機的紀錄放在一起，跟你的身分無關。

我們不收集姓名、電子郵件、電話、位置、聯絡人、照片、廣告識別碼，也沒有帳號系統。伺服器不記錄你的 IP 位址。

怎麼使用

只用來分析遊戲難度與平衡。不放廣告、不追蹤你在其他 App 或網站的行為、不賣也不分享給第三方。

傳送與保存

  • 紀錄經 HTTPS 加密傳送。傳送路徑經過 Cloudflare 的網路服務，Cloudflare 依其隱私權政策處理連線資料。
  • 沒有網路時，紀錄先留在手機裡，下次連上網再送。
  • 伺服器上的紀錄保存到不再需要做平衡分析為止。
  • 遊戲進度只存在你的手機上；刪除 App 會刪掉進度與還沒送出的紀錄，也就不會再上傳。

兒童

本遊戲含模擬賭博內容，App Store 分級為成人（18+，部分地區 17+），不以兒童為對象。

你的權利與聯絡方式

因為紀錄是匿名的，我們沒辦法從資料認出你是誰。有任何隱私問題或要求，請寄信到 support@ai-hanchi.com。

English

What we collect

To balance the game, it sends anonymous gameplay records to the developer's own server:

  • Gameplay: chosen character and skin, difficulty, each stage's score and result, gear bought and sold, play time, and similar.
  • Game version, operating system and device model (e.g. iPhone15,2).
  • A random ID generated when the app is installed, used only to group records from the same device. It is not linked to your identity.

We do not collect your name, email, phone number, location, contacts, photos or advertising identifier, and there are no accounts. The server does not record your IP address.

How we use it

Only to analyze difficulty and balance. No ads, no tracking across other apps or websites, and no selling or sharing with third parties.

Transmission and storage

  • Records are sent over HTTPS through Cloudflare's network, which handles connection data under its own privacy policy.
  • Without a connection, records stay on the device and are sent later.
  • Records on the server are kept only as long as needed for balance analysis.
  • Game progress is stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes your progress and any unsent records, and stops all uploads.

Children

The game contains simulated gambling, is rated for adults (18+, 17+ in some regions), and is not directed at children.

Your rights and contact

Because the records are anonymous, we cannot tell from the data who you are. For any privacy question or request, email support@ai-hanchi.com.

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
