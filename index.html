聯絡我們

遊戲有問題、發現 bug、或有任何建議，請寄信到：

support@ai-hanchi.com

來信請附上：手機型號、iOS 版本、遊戲版本（標題畫面戰績那一行的「版本」）與發生的狀況，有截圖更好。我們通常會在幾天內回覆。

常見問題

  • 這是賭博嗎？不是。遊戲內的錢與鋼珠都不能兌換成現金，也不能用真錢下注。
  • 進度存在哪裡？存在你的手機上。刪除 App 會一併刪除進度。
  • 沒有網路能玩嗎？可以，整個遊戲都能離線玩。
  • 怎麼換語言？標題畫面 →「設定」最上面。

English

Contact us

For bugs, problems or suggestions, email:

support@ai-hanchi.com

Please include your device model, iOS version, the game version (shown as "Version" in the stats line on the title screen) and what happened. Screenshots help. We usually reply within a few days.

FAQ

  • Is this gambling? No. In-game money and balls cannot be exchanged for cash, and you cannot bet real money.
  • Where is my progress saved? On your device. Deleting the app deletes your progress.
  • Can I play offline? Yes, the whole game works offline.
  • How do I change the language? Title screen → Settings, at the top.
